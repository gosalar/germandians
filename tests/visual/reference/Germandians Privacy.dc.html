Privacy note

This page explains what happens to the details you send us through this website or contact us with.

Last updated 11 November 2026

Who is responsible

Germandians is operated by [registered business name], [business postal address].

Email: thegermandians@gmail.com

What we collect

When you send an enquiry, we receive your name, WhatsApp number, any email address you choose to provide, the time of your message or submission, and your consent to be contacted.

If you use the grade check, the form includes the marks and profile answers you entered, plus the conversion when valid. Unanswered questions are omitted. Using the checker alone sends no profile details to us; the summary is shared only when you submit the form or send the email draft.

If you message us on WhatsApp, Instagram or by email, we receive whatever you send there, such as your transcripts or test scores.

What we use it for

We use your details only to reply to you and to carry out your profile review. We don't sell them, use them for advertising or add you to a mailing list.

The legal basis is your consent, which you give with the checkbox on the form (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), and the steps you ask us to take before we might work together (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

We don't use tracking or advertising cookies on this site.

Who else handles it

  • The form opens an email draft addressed to us. Nothing is sent until you press send in your email app. Your email provider and our Gmail service then handle the message.
  • [hosting provider] hosts this site and keeps short technical logs, such as IP addresses and access times, to keep it running.
  • Google provides our email (Gmail). Meta runs WhatsApp and Instagram, which we use when you message us there.

The Germandians team receives your enquiry to review it and reply on WhatsApp.

Some of these services may process data outside the EU, including in the USA.

How long we keep it

If we don't end up working together, we delete your details within [confirmed enquiry retention period] of your last message. If we do, we keep them for as long as we're helping you, plus any period the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the details we hold about you, to limit how we use them, or to send them to you in a portable format. You can also object to how we use them.

You can ask for deletion or withdraw your consent at any time by emailing thegermandians@gmail.com. This doesn't affect anything we did with your details before you withdrew it.

You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example [relevant data protection authority].
