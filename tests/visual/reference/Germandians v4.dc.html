Before work starts, your written quote states:

  • what changes the price
  • what counts as admission, including conditional, private and backup offers, and whether only your approved shortlist counts
  • whether there is one fee or a fee per admission
  • how many applications are covered

  • cancellation and deferral

From first call to first week in Germany

Four stages that overlap. The seven months below are one example of how they can fit together, not a fixed duration.

  1. From the start APS

    Foundation

    We evaluate your profile and start programme research, requirement checks and deadline planning with you. Alongside that we help you apply for APS, check whether you need dMAT and plan your IELTS preparation.

  2. Overlaps with Foundation

    Applications

    We shortlist universities with you, write your SOP with you and submit every application on the university portals. Where a programme needs a VPD from uni-assist, we request it early: the university application is a separate step with its own deadline.

  3. Overlaps with Applications Visa

    Pre-departure

    We help with your education loan, help you open your blocked account and prepare your visa documents and appointment. We plan these with you early and complete each one as your offers and documents arrive.

  4. Once you can travel Anmeldung

    Arrival

    We help with your housing applications before you travel, then take you through city registration (Anmeldung) and opening a bank account, and introduce you to seniors. Your written quote sets out what this support covers.

Housing preparation starts before you travel. Apply as soon as each provider opens applications: rooms are allocated from waiting lists and a place is not guaranteed. Keep private rooms and temporary accommodation in view as alternatives.Studierendenwerk München Oberbayern: housing FAQs · Last reviewed 12 November 2026

How we write your SOP with you

Your Statement of Purpose is the one place in the application where you explain your record in your own words, including the semesters that went badly.

Many consultancies generate SOPs with AI or reuse templates. We write yours by hand with you, over several weeks. Nothing is submitted until you've approved the final version.

What a rewrite looks like

Illustrative example. The student and details are invented.

Generic draft

Since my childhood I have always been passionate about mechanical engineering. I am a hardworking, dedicated and sincere student. Due to some personal reasons I had backlogs, but I cleared them all and I am now fully ready for a master's degree in Germany.

Rewritten with the student

In my fourth semester I failed six subjects while working evenings at my uncle's workshop after my father's surgery.1 I cleared every one of them over the next two semesters, and the workshop is where I learned to diagnose hydraulic faults, the problem my final-year project on pump efficiency set out to solve.2

  1. 1States the reason for the backlogs once, plainly, where the draft hid it behind "personal reasons".
  2. 2Links the hard semester to a specific technical interest and the final-year project, which leads into the course being applied for.

Two applicants, one university

Both applied to the same German technical university in the Winter 2025 cycle.

Each decision weighed the whole application: grades, course fit, documents and the SOP. The SOP is the part we write with you.

Affected degree fields: All B.Tech/B.Eng degrees; other engineering degrees; commerce, accounting, finance, economics; business/management; tourism, travel and hospitality. Integrated or dual degrees count if a component or major falls in these fields.
Who is exempt: Bachelor’s and PhD applicants, other degree fields, officially confirmed exchange, double-degree or university partnership programmes and students below 5 completed semesters of a 3-year degree or 7 of a 4-year degree are exempt under the current procedure.
